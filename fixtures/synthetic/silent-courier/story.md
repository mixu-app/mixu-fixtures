# The Silent Courier

## Scene 1 — The midnight delivery

At midnight, a cloaked figure slipped into the Old Watchhouse. The courier spoke
no words, their face concealed behind a heavy scarf and a dark waxcloth mantle.
The visitor deposited a sealed copper cylinder on the watch desk before Inspector
Clara and Constable Miller, then turned and vanished into the river fog before
either officer could bar the door.

## Scene 2 — The infirmary inquiry

At dawn, Inspector Clara visited St. Jude's Infirmary to trace the cylinder's
origin. She questioned Dr. Vance, the senior apothecary, regarding recent
irregularities in the hospital dispensary. Dr. Vance insisted he had spent the
entire night attending patients in the quarantined fever ward and had not once
set foot outside the infirmary gates.

## Scene 3 — The chemical trace

Returning to the watchhouse, Clara and Miller unsealed the cylinder. Inside lay
an account ledger exposing fraudulent hospital purchases, coated in a fine layer
of copperas dust. Miller observed that copperas was kept only in the hospital's
compounding stores, confirming the document came from within the infirmary, though
the identity of the masked messenger remained unknown.

## Scene 4 — The drying mantle

Clara returned to the infirmary and searched the compounding laboratory. Behind
the boiler hung a dripping waxcloth mantle smelling of river mist, its cuff
streaked with copperas. Dr. Vance surrendered the missing cylinder key and
confessed that he had delivered the papers himself, using the courier disguise to
evade the hospital governors.
