## Authorship and generation method

This fixture is a 100% original synthetic mystery scenario authored specifically
for the Mixu spoiler regression suite. It was designed and written by the
project maintainers as a controlled micro-mystery testing knowledge-horizon
filtering against delayed disguise revelations, physical trace corroboration,
and alibi invalidation.

The narrative text (`story.md`) and graph specification (`fixture.json`) were
authored together to establish deterministic verification criteria across four
horizon ordinals:

1. **Horizon 1 (Scene 1):** Introduces Inspector Clara, Constable Miller, and the
   unidentified masked courier delivering a sealed copper cylinder.
2. **Horizon 2 (Scene 2):** Introduces Dr. Vance at the infirmary with an alibi of
   tending fever ward patients all night.
3. **Horizon 3 (Scene 3):** Reveals the ledger inside the cylinder and the chemical
   trace (copperas dust) linking the delivery to the compounding laboratory,
   while keeping the courier's true identity hidden.
4. **Horizon 4 (Scene 4):** Locates the wet mantle and missing key, leading to Dr.
   Vance's confession and identity merge with the courier, which contradicts the
   previous alibi.

The text and annotations were generated entirely de novo without reference to or
derivation from any published or copyrighted fictional works.
