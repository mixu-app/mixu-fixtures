# The Locked Stair

## Scene 1 — The register

The guesthouse keep had one room left. The man signed the register as
"Mr. Ishida", paid in advance, and asked not to be disturbed before noon. He
left a broken umbrella in the lobby stand and carried nothing else upstairs.

## Scene 2 — The stair

At eleven the lamp on the stair went out. The keep, coming up with a candle,
passed a man in a grey coat descending quietly, one hand on the rail. She did
not see his face and did not think to ask his name.

## Scene 3 — The grey coat

In the morning the keep said Mr. Ishida had never left his room; the grey coat
on the peg was his, and the stair lamp had simply run out of oil. The register,
the coat and the umbrella all belonged to the same quiet guest.

## Scene 4 — The porter's account

The porter disagreed. He had been at the door at midnight and had let a man in
a grey coat out into the rain, and the man had not come back. The keep repeated
that the coat was still on the peg. Neither of them had checked the room.
