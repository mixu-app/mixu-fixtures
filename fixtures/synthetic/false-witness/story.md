## Scene 1 — The Midnight Chime

A shadow breached the high terrace of Highgate Manor just as the hall clock chimed
midnight. Within minutes, the display cabinet in Lord Harrington's library stood
forced, its velvet cushion bare: the prized golden chronometer had vanished.
Detective Bradley examined the lock and identified the distinct rotary tool marks
of an elusive London burglar known only as the Clockmaker. No prints remained,
and no servant reported hearing footfalls on the marble floor.

## Scene 2 — The Housekeeper's Accusation

At daybreak, Detective Bradley summoned the estate staff to the morning room.
The head housekeeper, Mrs. Gable, stepped forward with urgent testimony. She
insisted under oath that she had looked through the scullery window at midnight and
spotted Thomas Cole, the estate gardener, sprinting towards the conservatory
with a heavy velvet pouch. Furthermore, she claimed she watched Cole hurl a brass
skeleton key into the lily pond before disappearing down the gravel drive.

## Scene 3 — The Arrest of Julian Price

Bradley traced the specialized rotary tools to a precision workshop in Southwark.
There, the detective arrested a craftsman registered as Julian Price, uncovering
the golden chronometer wrapped in chamois beneath the workbench. Under questioning,
Julian Price admitted he was the notorious Clockmaker. Price formally confessed
that he had planned and executed the Highgate burglary entirely alone, entering
through the unlocked library balcony without assistance from any estate servant.

## Scene 4 — The Retracted Statement

Armed with Price's confession, Bradley confronted Mrs. Gable in the manor parlor.
The detective presented the precinct holding-cell logbook, proving Thomas Cole
had been detained for disorderly conduct three hours before the theft and had
remained locked in custody all night. Confronted with unshakeable evidence,
Mrs. Gable broke into tears and retracted her entire deposition, confessing she had
fabricated the accusation because Cole had threatened to expose her systemic theft
of estate silver.
